Principal Investigator 연구책임자

Yoo Seong Choi 최유성

Professor, Department of Chemical Engineering and Applied Chemistry
Chungnam National University

Prof. Choi’s research focuses mainly on proteins and how their sequences, structures, and interactions shape assembly and function. His major research area include biomolecular condensates and enzyme engineering, with applications in functional biomaterials and bioprocesses.
