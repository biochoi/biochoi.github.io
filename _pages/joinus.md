We are always looking for motivated students and researchers who are excited about biomolecular engineering.
<br>

## Graduate Students (M.S. / Ph.D.)

We welcome applicants with backgrounds in chemical engineering, biotechnology, biochemistry, or related fields. Prior research experience is helpful but not required — curiosity and persistence matter more.

Prospective students should contact Prof. Choi by email with a brief statement of research interests and a CV.
<br>

## Undergraduate Researchers

Undergraduate students at CNU interested in hands-on research experience are encouraged to reach out. Participation in individual research projects is available throughout the academic year.
<br>

## Postdoctoral Researchers

Inquiries from postdoctoral candidates are welcome. Please include a CV, a summary of previous research, and contact information for references.
